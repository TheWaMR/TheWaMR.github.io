Me

Howdy!

I’m William Rohren, a mechanical engineering student at Texas A&M, graduating in December 2026. I’m looking for a full-time structures, test, or design engineering role after graduation.

Most of my work sits where mechanical and electrical meet: FEA-validated aircraft structures for SAE Aero, strain-gauge data acquisition for my SwRI capstone, custom PCBs, and keeping the machines running at the MEEN Rapid Prototyping Studio (RPS), where I work as a student technician.

Tools I use most: SolidWorks, Ansys, Femap/Nastran, Altium, KiCAD, and Python. At the RPS: the CO2 laser cutters, the 3D printers, and the CNC plasma cutter.
